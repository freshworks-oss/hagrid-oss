# Glossary 

Below is the list of words used by `devs` &  `members` and their meaning. As list of these keywords may go longer, please 
use search bar to search for the word in this glossary. 

1. dev - Any person who uses `Hagrid` to develop connector
2. member - Any person who is the part of core Hagrid team. `dev` can become members once they have expertise.
3. `stepList` - It refers to the `list` data structure that is created and used by `Hagrid` to save the data that it has fetched
   for this step. For instace - for `Fbuser` hagrid may fetch 20 users then it will save it in `stepList` of this step.
